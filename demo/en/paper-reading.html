Original synthetic chart. Group values are invented for interface demonstration; no real paper is reproduced.

Question and design

Compare groups A and B on one measurement scale. Each bar is a group summary; these invented values demonstrate the interface, not a biological experiment.

Legend and plotted elements

Visual elementMeaning
Orange bar / blue barGroup A / group B, respectively.
Bar height and number above itThe invented measurement value, in arbitrary demonstration units; not a sample count or uncertainty estimate.

From observation to inference

Group A is 60 and B is 35, so the observed summary for A is 25 units higher. The same scale permits this descriptive comparison.

No replicate distribution or uncertainty is supplied. The figure therefore cannot establish a stable population difference or a causal mechanism.

Limits and next question

Next ask whether the difference repeats, then design a comparison that distinguishes possible causes. The second synthetic panel illustrates another observation; it is not an independent real-world validation.

Know what the evidence does not establish

Different bar heights are an observation. A causal explanation requires a design that distinguishes alternatives. These numbers are invented for interface demonstration.

Look up a concept without losing your place

Control: what is the reference for this comparison?

A control supplies a reference for interpreting a measurement. In this demo, A and B share a measurement scale. In a real paper, you must also check whether background differences, treatments, and sampling support the proposed comparison.

Plotted unit: what does each point or bar represent?

Identify whether an element represents a sample, gene, genomic region, or summary before interpreting a pattern.

Why does the next step follow?

Question: do the groups differ?
↓ Compare the same measured quantity
Observation: A has a higher summary value
↓ Is the difference stable, and why does it occur?
Next step: replication and a design that tests alternatives

A reader's perspective
